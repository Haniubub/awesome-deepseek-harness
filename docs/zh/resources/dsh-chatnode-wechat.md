## 详细介绍

**Chat with, monitor, and approve your DSH agents from WeChat.** A [DeepSeek Harness](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness) bundle that connects a DSH profile to a WeChat personal account over Tencent's unofficial **iLink bot gateway** (`ilinkai.weixin.qq.com`) — the same mechanism hermes-agent and OpenClaw use. Text and images go both ways (inbound images are downloaded, decrypted and handed to the agent; `/send` sends an image back), session targeting works with `/sessions /use /new
