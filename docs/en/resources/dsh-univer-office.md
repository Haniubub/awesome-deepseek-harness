Give DeepSeek Harness a real office environment.  Univer Office Plugin brings spreadsheets, docs, slides, canvases, relational tables, and more into one runtime — with connected data, validation, versioned changes, and isolated worktrees for multi-agent collaboration.

## About

English · [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) `dsh-univer-office` is the Univer office plugin for DeepSeek Harness (DSH). Tell the agent what you need and it can create or edit spreadsheets, documents, presentations, multidimensional tables, and canvases, or work with existing Excel, Word, and PowerPoint files. Every change is verified and stays in the conversation for you to preview, approve, or discard. After installation, describe the result you want in natural language.
