## About

**Your DeepSeek Harness agent rings your actual phone.** It asks the question out loud, you answer out loud, and what you said goes straight back into the run. Every other reachability plugin sends you a notification. This one places a phone call. Your phone rings through CallKit like any other call, you pick it up, a voice reads the agent's question, you say "yes, ship it, but hold the migration", and the transcript of that sentence is what the agent reads next.
