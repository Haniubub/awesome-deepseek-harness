## About

An open source coding agent for your terminal — bring your own model. Codewhale started as a native experience for DeepSeek. It has since grown into a community-driven project: one coding harness that fits a growing international community and supports as many models and providers as possible — open models first, hosted or local, none privileged over the rest.
