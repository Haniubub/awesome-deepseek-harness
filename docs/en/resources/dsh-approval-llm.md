## About

**Model-based permission approval (approve-for-me) for [DeepSeek Harness](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness).** A community plugin that adds a **"model approval" permission mode** (approve-for-me) to DeepSeek Harness: in that mode, `approval/request` asks are answered by a **separate reviewer model** instead of a human — the reviewer decides `ALLOW / DENY / ESCALATE`, and the request only reaches a human when the reviewer cannot decide or fails.
